# WAN stages W0, W1, W2 (built and proven by LAB-048)

Each stage was cold-boot tested in its own CML lab (W0 = the LAB-048 start state, W1 and W2 each 10 nodes BOOTED in 83-84 s, OSPF FULL and branch-to-HQ pings after about 1-2 min). **PROV-PE1 and PROV-INET1 are read-only provider background**: unhardened, no faults, never changed by a lab. LAB-050 adds W3 and LAB-052 adds W4 (11 nodes each, below).

**Profile:** WAN full = 10 nodes: HQ-CORE1 (stub), HQ-WAN1, PROV-PE1, PROV-INET1, BR1-RTR1, BR2-RTR1 (`iol-xe`), BR1-ACC1 (`ioll2-xe`), HQ-PC1, BR1-PC1, BR2-PC1 (`alpine`). Cabling, addressing and loopbacks: as in LAB-048's LAB.md. Loopbacks Lo201/202 (WAN1, BR1) and Lo201/203 (BR2) exist from W0 as /32s in the provider /29s and are unused before W3/E4. Hosts: HQ-PC1 10.10.10.101, BR1-PC1 10.21.10.101, BR2-PC1 10.22.10.101.

## Measured behaviour
- (a) PASS: OSPF over IKEv2 GRE-over-IPsec with hello 5 / dead 20 / cost 100: FULL on both tunnels, `Tunnel transport MTU 1414` (plain GRE 1476), DF ping through the tunnel 1400 passes and 1401 fails, SYN through the tunnel carries `mss 1360`.
- (b) PARTIAL: adding `tunnel protection` to a running W1 tunnel keeps the interface and OSPF configuration (adjacencies re-form once the SAs are up). **GRE `keepalive 5 3` does not work on a protected tunnel** on iol-xe: `line protocol is down`, `Tunnel linestate evaluation down - keepalive down`, SA READY, ESP counters rising, `%CRYPTO-4-RECVD_PKT_NOT_IPSEC ... prot=47` on the receiver (the peer's keepalive reply leaves unencrypted). Fallback applied: no keepalive in W2; OSPF dead (20 s) and DPD detect failures.
- (c) PASS: `eq isakmp`, `eq non500-isakmp`, `esp` and the ICMP type keywords are accepted; counters rise; denied packets from INET1 log `%SEC-6-IPACCESSLOGDP`.
- (d) measured failover (device-clock; table in LAB-048 SOLUTION.md): cable pull of BR1-INET1: pulled side 2-6 s, far side (HQ-WAN1) OSPF dead 14-15 s in W2 (13.8-16.9 s in W1), tunnel line 16-22 s, recovery after link-up 8-16 s. Silent failure of BR2-INET1 (W2, 6 runs): OSPF dead 18-23 s, tunnel line down 21-30 s, recovery after link start 4-12 s.
- Note: `clear crypto session` on one end only makes the tunnel flap about every 3.5 s for about 80 s before it settles; use `shutdown`/`no shutdown` on the tunnel instead.
- Encapsulation: capture on the BR2-INET1 link in W2 shows only ESP (protocol 50) for tunnel traffic, IKE_SA_INIT (exch 34), IKE_AUTH (35), no protocol 47; NTP stays in the clear on UDP 123 (underlay, by design).

## W3 (built and proven by LAB-050): 11 nodes
Adds BR1-PC2 (alpine, VLAN 40 on BR1-ACC1 e0/2, 10.21.40.102, gateway 10.21.40.1) to the W2 profile. Cold-booted as day-0 in its own CML lab: 11 nodes BOOTED in 83 s, 7 OSPF neighbours FULL on WAN1 and 5 IKEv2 SAs READY (3 new: `.145-.153` MGMT, `.145-.161` MGMT, `.146-.154` OT) after about 2 min; the tenant matrix (users reach neither MGMT nor OT; BR1-PC2 reaches only 10.10.40.1; HQ management reaches the three branch devices) held. Platform behaviour: see LAB-050's SOLUTION.md. Per-tenant tunnel timers as W2 (5/20, cost 100, `ip mtu 1400`, `ip tcp adjust-mss 1360`, no `keepalive`). BR1-ACC1 loses its NTP path (its gateway is in VRF MGMT): not a check target. Failover of BR1's three tunnels (cable pull of BR1-INET1): OSPF dead 12.5-17.2 s, line down 16.8-23.8 s, tunnels FULL again 10-24 s after the link returns; corporate traffic falls back to the provider /16 in 17-20 s, MGMT and OT to BR1 stay down (no backup).

## W4 (built and proven by LAB-052): 11 nodes
W3 plus operations features on BR1-RTR1 and HQ-WAN1 (table row above). Cold-booted as day-0 in its own CML lab (11 nodes BOOTED in 84 s; tracks 30/31 Up and tunnels FULL after about 1 min; EF to HQ takes the provider line, ordinary traffic the tunnel; the W3 matrix sample holds). Provider devices unchanged. Measured: CML link conditioning **bandwidth works on an IOL link** (2000 kb/s: unshaped 5 Mb/s UDP arrives at 1.78 Mb/s payload) and is exported; inner DSCP is copied through GRE into the ESP outer header, so a policy on the physical interface classifies protected-tunnel traffic; a shaper with LLQ reorders ESP beyond the default 64-packet anti-replay window (`%CRYPTO-4-PKT_REPLAY_ERR`, best effort starved), fixed by `replay window-size 1024` on both ends (new SAs); IP SLA needs `threshold` <= `timeout`. Failover times: silent failure of BR1-PE1 moves voice to the tunnel in 6.8-9.6 s (BR1 to HQ) and 9.1-11.9 s (HQ to BR1) and back 32-39 s after the link returns (`delay up 30`).

## Restart rule
Cold start: all nodes BOOTED in about 85 s; OSPF FULL and tunnels after about 1 min; NTP `sys.peer` on the branch clients after 15-30 min (check last; many e0/0 shutdowns reset BR1-RTR1's poll).
